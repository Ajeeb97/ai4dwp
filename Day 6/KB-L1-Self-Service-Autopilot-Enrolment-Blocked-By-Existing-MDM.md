# Easy Help: Device Setup Fails Because of Existing Company Management

## Version Header
- Version: 1.0
- Date: 11/08/2026
- Status: Draft

## What this is
If your company device setup (Autopilot) fails, it may be because the device still has an older company management profile from a past setup.

Your data is safe. This is a setup-link issue, not data loss.

## What you can do now
1. Do not keep retrying setup many times.
2. Make sure the device is connected to the internet.
3. Take a photo of the error screen if possible.
4. Contact Service Desk and report:
- Device setup failed during company enrolment.
- Mention error code if shown (example: `0x80180014`).

## What Service Desk may ask you to do
1. Stay online for a short remote session, or
2. Bring the device for quick support so old management linkage can be removed.

## Before you contact Service Desk
Please provide:
- Your full name
- Device name (if known)
- Device serial number (if available)
- Time the failure happened
- Exact error code shown on screen
- Whether this device was used before in the company

## When to escalate immediately
Escalate immediately if:
- You are blocked from first-day setup.
- You see repeated setup failure after restart.
- Setup fails with same error each retry.
