## What to do

If sign-in is slow or shortcuts are missing:

1. Do not retry, uninstall the app, or delete profile files.
2. Write down your name, device, time, and any error message.
3. If you can sign in, save work and contact the Service Desk.
4. If you cannot sign in, use the alternate workflow and contact the Service Desk from another device.
5. Do not open unexpected Copilot content. Report the matter name or file link privately.

IT will collect evidence, test a repair, and decide whether to expand fixes to other devices. Keep your device available and follow Service Desk instructions.

**Unexpected Copilot content is a security concern, not a support issue.** Report it privately to IT immediately.
