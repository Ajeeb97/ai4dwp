## What happened

Some colleagues on Floor 6 have had trouble signing in or have waited a long time for their computer to become ready. Some desktop shortcuts are also missing. The problem appeared after a new document-management application was installed on Friday afternoon. We are investigating that timing because it is the clearest shared change, but timing alone does not prove the application caused the problem.

One colleague also reported that Copilot showed unexpected information from a client matter. This is a security concern because it could mean the person had access to information they should not have had, even if Copilot was behaving normally. We are checking the person's permission and the computer records; we have not concluded that information was exposed or that Copilot made an error.

## What we are doing

We have stopped any further installation while we investigate. We are comparing computers that are affected with computers that are working, because that will show whether the new installation is shared by the affected group. We are testing a carefully controlled repair on a small number of computers. Separately, we are checking the colleague's actual permission to the reported matter with the people responsible for security and privacy.

## What colleagues should do

If you can sign in, continue normal approved work and contact the Service Desk if the delay or missing shortcuts continues. If you cannot sign in, stop repeated attempts and report your name, computer name, time, and any message shown. This gives the support team enough detail to compare your computer with the working ones.

Do not uninstall the new application or delete files from your computer. Do not open, copy, forward, or discuss unexpected client information shown by Copilot. Report that privately to IT or your manager, including the matter or file name only through the approved channel.

## What remains open

We are still confirming whether the new application caused the computer problems, whether the missing shortcuts have been removed or only moved, and whether the Copilot report reflects permission that already existed or permission that should not exist. We will provide the next update at the time agreed by the incident lead, even if the investigation is still in progress.
