@file:DWP_Personal_AI_Usage_Charter.md You are a DWP service desk analyst. In the same response, generate a structured triage summary for each ticket below.

- Summary (one line)
- Impact (who/how many/ business urgency)

Rules:
- Do not invent facts.
- Do not invent error codes, registry keys, or KB numbers.
