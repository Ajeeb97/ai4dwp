# Easy Help: Black Screen After Sign-In

## Version Header
- Version: 1.0
- Date: 07/08/2026
- Status: Draft

## What this is
If you sign in and your work screen stays black, use the steps below. This is a known problem and our help team is already familiar with it.

Your files and work data are safe. This issue affects what you see on screen, not your saved documents.

## What you can do now
1. Sign out of your work screen.
2. Wait 30 seconds.
3. Sign in again.
4. Wait up to 60 seconds after sign-in to see if your normal work screen appears.
5. If the screen is still black, sign out again.
6. Restart your laptop or computer.
7. Sign in to your work screen one more time.
8. If the problem continues, stop trying to sign in again and contact the help team.

## Before you contact the help team
Please keep these details ready. You can copy this list into your support request:
- Your full name
- Your work sign-in name
- Best contact number
- Office location or home location
- Time the issue started (include your local time)
- How many times you tried to sign in
- What happened each time (for example: black screen for more than 60 seconds)
- Whether restarting your laptop or computer helped
- If possible, a photo of the black screen

## Contact the help team if...
Contact the help team if the screen is still black after the steps above, if you cannot work, or if this happens again on the same day.
