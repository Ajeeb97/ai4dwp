# Easy Help: Finance Shared Drive Not Opening

## Version Header
- Version: 1.0
- Date: 07/08/2026
- Status: Draft

## What this is
If your Finance shared folder is missing or will not open, follow the steps below.

Your files are safe. This issue affects access, not the saved data.

## What you can do now
1. Save your work in open apps.
2. Sign out of your work account on your laptop.
3. Wait 30 seconds.
4. Sign in again.
5. Open File Explorer and try the Finance shared folder again.
6. If you work from home, connect to your company network access app, then try again.
7. Restart your laptop or computer once.
8. Try the Finance shared folder one more time.
9. If it still does not open, contact the Service Desk.

## Before you contact the Service Desk
Copy these details into your support request:
- Your full name
- Your work sign-in name
- Best contact number
- Office or home location
- Time the problem started (your local time)
- The folder path you tried to open (if known)
- The exact message shown (for example: Access denied)
- Whether sign-out/sign-in helped
- Whether restart helped

## Contact the Service Desk if...
Contact the Service Desk if the folder is still not opening after these steps, if you cannot do your work, or if this happens again later today.
