Subject: FinBridge Connect v3.1 deployment for Finance this week

Hello Finance team,

This week we will begin deploying `FinBridge Connect v3.1` to Finance-managed Windows 11 devices. Finance is the first business group in scope because the application is required for priority business activity this week.

What to expect:
- Deployment starts with a limited pilot group, then expands to the wider Finance group after validation.
- The application may install automatically through our managed software service.
- Some users may be prompted to close the application if an earlier version is open during upgrade.

What you need to do:
- Leave your device powered on and connected to the corporate network or VPN during the deployment window.
- Report any launch issues, performance issues, or workflow failures to the Service Desk and mention `FinBridge Connect v3.1`.

Known note:
- A small number of older devices may require separate handling if performance does not meet the minimum standard.

Support:
- Service Desk: use the standard support channel and include your device name and a screenshot if available.
- Priority escalation: Finance-critical issues will be triaged immediately during the Week 1 rollout window.

Thank you,
Endpoint Engineering
