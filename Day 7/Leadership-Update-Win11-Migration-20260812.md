# Win11 Migration – Leadership Update
**Date:** 2026-08-12
**Prepared by:** DWP IT Service Desk
**Audience:** Senior leadership / non-technical management

---

The Windows 11 migration for FinBridge staff has, overall, gone well. In the first week, a number of colleagues experienced difficulties — including being unable to sign in, files appearing to go missing, and problems accessing shared drives. These issues affected a meaningful number of people and were treated as priorities. All of them have since been resolved, and the majority of staff reported in week two that their experience is now smooth, fast, and reliable. The feedback we received shows that the migration delivered a real improvement in day-to-day performance, and several colleagues specifically thanked the team for a clean transition.

Two issues remain open and are being actively managed. A floor-3 printer has failed to reconnect automatically since the migration began and, despite repeated attempts, has not yet been fully fixed — the team is now committed to delivering a permanent resolution this week rather than further workarounds. Separately, Finance staff have started experiencing Microsoft Excel freezing and closing unexpectedly when working with large budget files, which in some cases has caused loss of unsaved work. This has been identified as a direct result of a security configuration change applied last weekend and is our current top priority. We are investigating the specific setting responsible and expect to have a resolution in place shortly. Impacted Finance colleagues have been advised to save their work frequently in the meantime. Both issues are being tracked and we will provide an update to affected teams by end of week.
