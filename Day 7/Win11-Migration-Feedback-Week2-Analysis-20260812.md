## Top-Ranked Issues This Week

### Rank 1 — Excel Crashes on Large Files

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Theme** | Excel Crashes on Large Files |
| **Count** | 9 comments |
| **Severity** | Blocker |
| **Status** | New — not present in Week 1 feedback |

**Why it ranks #1:**
Nine users — concentrated in the Finance team — report Excel crashing when opening files over approximately 10MB, with unsaved work being lost each time. Data loss combined with Finance team exposure makes this the highest-priority new issue. It is likely linked to a memory, driver, or add-in compatibility change introduced by the Win11 migration.

**Representative quotes:**
- "Excel crash is really disruptive, losing unsaved work each time."
- "Excel crashing has become a real productivity problem for finance team."

**For your manager:**
> Nine Finance users are losing unsaved work due to Excel crashes on large files — this is a new post-migration Blocker that needs same-day investigation, likely a compatibility or memory configuration issue.

## Key Observations

1. **Week 1 remediation was effective** — all three Week 1 Blockers are confirmed resolved by returning users. The proactive all-staff notice and self-service guidance appear to have reduced ticket volume.
2. **A new Blocker has emerged** — Excel large-file crashes are concentrated in Finance and involve data loss. This must be treated as a priority incident, not a background defect.
3. **The printer issue is degrading trust** — volume has grown from 6 to 10 comments and the tone has shifted from frustrated to resigned. Two weeks without a fix requires an owner and a committed resolution date.
