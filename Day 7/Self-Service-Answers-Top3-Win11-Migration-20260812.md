# Self-Service Answers – Win11 Migration Top Issues
**Date:** 2026-08-12
**Audience:** FinBridge end users

---

## 1. Can't sign in / account locked out

Wait 15 minutes and try signing in again — accounts unlock automatically after a short time. If you know your password is correct, try signing in from a different device (such as your mobile or a colleague's machine) to check whether the issue is with your account or your specific device.

**Contact the service desk if:** you are still locked out after 15 minutes, or this has happened more than once this week.
**Tell them:** your name, your username, the time it happened, and what message appeared on screen.

---

## 2. OneDrive files appear missing

Open OneDrive by clicking the cloud icon in the bottom-right corner of your screen. If it shows a red or yellow warning, click it and select **Resume syncing**. Wait two minutes and check your files again. If the icon is not visible, search for "OneDrive" in the Start menu and open it.

**Contact the service desk if:** files are still missing after syncing has finished, or you need a specific file urgently today.
**Tell them:** your name, the name of the missing file or folder, and roughly when you last saw it.

---

## 3. Can't open the shared S: drive

Click **Start**, type `\\dwp-fileserver\finance` into the search bar, and press Enter — this can reconnect you to the drive directly. If that does not work, sign out of your computer and sign back in, then try opening the S: drive again from File Explorer.

**Contact the service desk if:** you still cannot open the drive after trying both steps above, or you are blocked from completing time-sensitive work.
**Tell them:** your name, your team, what you see when you try to open the drive (error message if any), and how urgent your deadline is.
