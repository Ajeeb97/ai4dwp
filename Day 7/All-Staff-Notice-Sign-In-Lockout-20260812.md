# All-Staff Notice – Sign-In and Account Lockout Issue
**Date:** 2026-08-12
**From:** IT Service Desk
**To:** All FinBridge Staff

---

**Subject: Known Issue – Some users unable to sign in this morning**

We are aware that a number of colleagues are experiencing difficulties signing in or finding their accounts locked following the Windows 11 migration. We are actively investigating and working to resolve this as quickly as possible.

**While we work on a fix, please try the following:**

- Wait 15 minutes and try signing in again — accounts unlock automatically after a short time.
- If possible, try signing in from a different device, such as your mobile or a colleague's machine.

If you are still unable to sign in after 15 minutes, or this has happened more than once today, please contact the service desk and have the following ready: your name, your username, the time it occurred, and any message shown on screen.

We will provide a further update by **14:00 today**. We appreciate your patience — the team is on this and will keep you informed as the situation develops.
