# Win11 Migration – FinBridge Staff Feedback: Top 3 Action Ranking
**Date:** 2026-08-12
**Analyst:** DWP Analyst
**Source:** 50 post-migration end-user comments, FinBridge staff

---

## Ranking Methodology

Themes are weighted by both **volume** (number of affected users) and **impact** (Blocker / Friction / Minor / Positive). A single Blocker comment outranks multiple Minor comments. All three top-ranked themes are Blockers — users cannot complete their work.

---

## Rank 1 — Account Lockout & AVD Sign-In Failure

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Theme** | Account Lockout & AVD Sign-In Failure |
| **Count** | 7 comments |
| **Severity** | Blocker |

**Why it ranks #1:**
Highest volume among all Blocker themes. Seven users cannot sign in to AVD at all — some locked out repeatedly across multiple days and for hours at a time. This is the largest single group of users who are completely unable to work. The recurring nature (third time this week for some) indicates a systemic identity or policy issue, not a one-off.

**For your manager:**
> Seven FinBridge users have been fully locked out of AVD since the Win11 migration, some repeatedly across multiple days — this is our highest-priority incident and requires immediate escalation to identity/IAM.

## Rank 2 — OneDrive File Loss / Sync Errors

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Theme** | OneDrive File Loss / Sync Errors |
| **Count** | 4 comments |
| **Severity** | Blocker |

**Why it ranks #2:**
While lower in volume than Rank 1, the nature of the impact elevates this. Users report files appearing entirely missing, with urgent deadlines (Q1 report, end-of-day meetings). Potential data loss during a migration carries reputational and compliance risk that demands same-day response regardless of count.

**For your manager:**
> Four users are reporting missing OneDrive files with urgent business deadlines today — we need to confirm whether data is genuinely lost or a sync/profile path issue before end of day.

## Rank 3 — Shared Drive (S:) Access Denied

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Theme** | Shared Drive (S:) Access Denied |
| **Count** | 3 comments |
| **Severity** | Blocker |

**Why it ranks #3:**
Three users cannot access the Finance shared drive at all, including one explicitly blocked from completing month-end financial reports. Although lower in volume than the Floor 3 printer issue (6 comments, Friction), a Blocker outweighs Friction — these users have no workaround and finance deadlines make delay costly.

**For your manager:**
> Three Finance users have had no access to the S: shared drive since migration, blocking month-end reporting — this is likely a permissions group misconfiguration and should be resolved today.
