## New Theme in Week 2 — Not Present in Week 1

### 🔴 Excel Crashes on Large Files
**Week 2:** Blocker | 9 comments (IDs: 4, 8, 13, 16, 20, 24, 30, 33, 37)

Users report Excel freezing and crashing specifically when opening large files (noted as files over 10MB). Unsaved work is being lost. Issue is concentrated in the Finance team and explicitly described as a new problem since the migration.

> "Excel crash is really disruptive, losing unsaved work each time." (ID 13)
> "New Excel crash issue -- happens specifically with files over 10MB." (ID 33)

This is the highest-priority action item for Week 2. It did not exist in Week 1 feedback and may indicate a memory, add-in, or compatibility issue introduced by the Win11 upgrade.
